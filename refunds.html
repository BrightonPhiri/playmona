Refund policy

When we give your money back, and how to ask. Last updated 24 October 2026.

Draft. This policy describes how refunds work today. It has not had a legal review yet.

In short

  • If a payment went wrong, we fix it or refund it.
  • Bought by mistake? Tell us within 48 hours. If you have not used it yet, we refund it.
  • Plus time or a game you have already used is not refunded.

We refund you when

  • You paid but did not get what you bought, and we cannot fix it within 3 working days.
  • You were charged twice for the same thing, or charged a different amount from the price shown.
  • You bought something by mistake (for example the wrong plan), told us within 48 hours of paying, and have not played a Plus game or the game you bought since. We can also swap it for the plan you meant to buy.
  • The law where you live gives you a right to a refund.

We do not refund

  • Plus time or a bought game that you have used, including time left on a pass you have stopped using.
  • Purchases on an account we suspended or closed for breaking the Terms of Service.
  • Plus or games given as a gift or prize. They have no money value.

A Plus pass never renews by itself, so there is nothing to cancel: when the time you bought runs out, it simply ends.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Find the invoice number (it starts with PM-) in Settings, then Account, then Payment history, or on the Plus page.
  2. Email support@playmona.com with the invoice number, your PlayMona username and what went wrong.
  3. We reply within 3 working days.

How you get the money back

We send refunds back the way you paid (EcoCash, InnBucks, OneMoney, Omari, SmileCash or card) through our payment provider, Linkwa, in US dollars. It usually arrives within 10 working days of us agreeing the refund, depending on your bank or mobile money provider. When we refund a Plus pass or a game, it is removed from your account.

Payment problems

If a payment shows as pending, wait a few minutes: Plus starts on its own once Linkwa confirms the payment. If money left your account and nothing changed after an hour, email us with the time you paid and the payment reference from your EcoCash, bank or Linkwa message.
