Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Every game is free, with no ads.

Why do I need an account?

Your account holds your profile, saved players, stats and settings, so they are there on any device you use. It also gives you a name in online rooms.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once you have signed in on a device. Games that run on one screen keep working offline, and your progress syncs when you are back online. Online rooms need a connection.

What do I need to play?

A web browser on a phone, tablet, laptop or smart TV. Nothing to install, though you can add PlayMona to your home screen.
