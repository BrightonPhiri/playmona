Privacy

What we keep about you, where it lives, and how to delete it.

Draft. This page describes how the app works today. It has not had a legal review yet.

Your account

To play you need a PlayMona account. When you create one we store:

  • your email address (or your Google account's email, if you sign in with Google)
  • your username, display name and chosen character
  • a password managed by Google Firebase Authentication. PlayMona never sees or stores your password.

Your username is public: other players can look it up, and it is used to sign in with a username instead of an email. To make that work, the email linked to a username can be read by anyone who knows the username.

Your game data

So your progress follows you between devices, these are saved to your account: your saved players (names, characters, colours), teams, favourite games, recent games, scores and medals, and your game settings. Player photos never leave the device they were added on.

A copy is also kept in your browser so games work offline. Display settings such as theme and text size stay on each device.

Online rooms

When you join an online room, the other players in that room see your display name and character, and the room holds your answers and score while the game runs. Voice chat, if you switch it on, connects you straight to the other players' devices. Voice is not recorded or stored.

Who handles the data

Accounts and saved data are held by Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database). There are no ads, no advertising trackers and no analytics in PlayMona.

Children

PlayMona is made for all ages. Younger players should play on a parent or guardian's account, adding themselves as saved players, rather than creating their own account.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account: Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This removes your profile, saved players, stats and username from our servers.
  • Reset your progress: Settings, then Privacy, then Reset. This clears your saved players, teams, favourites and stats on every device, and keeps your account.
  • Sign out: your saved data stays in this browser so you can play offline next time. To remove it from a shared device, clear the site's data in your browser settings.
