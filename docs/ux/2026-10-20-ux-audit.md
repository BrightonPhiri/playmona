# Family Fun: UI/UX audit and redesign plan

## Context

Brighton asked for an honest, world-class UI/UX audit of Family Fun, followed by the redesign it calls for. Family Fun is a vanilla ES5 game hub with no build step and 45 games in 10 categories. It works offline and has Firebase rooms for cross-device play. Scope confirmed with Brighton:

- Commit the current uncommitted work first as a checkpoint.
- Implement everything, including the in-game UIs of all 45 games.
- Keep the 7-language picker, but mark non-English languages as partial ("Beta: menus only").

Evidence: I read `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/app.js`, `js/registry.js` (GameFlow and shell), `js/navigation.js`, `js/core.js` and `js/i18n.js`, and checked how the game files style themselves. I also ran the app at 1024×768 and 375×812 and launched Family Quiz through to gameplay.

---

## 1. Overall rating: 5.5 / 10. Charming but not yet world-class

| Area | Score | One-line verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Visual identity | 7 | The painted "game-night living room" art is distinctive. Keep it. |
| Mobile-first | 4 | The header uses about 26% of a phone screen, and the hero fills the first screen with no games visible. |
| Navigation & IA | 4 | 6 top-level tabs show as icons only on phones. Favorites and Scores are thin pages. |
| Discovery | 4 | No search, no player-count or time filters, and a hardcoded featured list. |
| Launch flow | 4 | Card → Intro → Setup modal → game. "Start" and "Settings" open the same screen. |
| Accessibility | 3.5 | Contrast fails, arrow keys are hijacked inside inputs, a fake "pre-selected" answer appears, and modals aren't real dialogs. |
| Consistency | 4 | Emoji and SVG icons are mixed, button labels are ALL CAPS, and about 300 inline-style sites have hardcoded colours. |
| Performance | 6 | 27 image 404s on every Home render, heavy `backdrop-filter`, infinite animations. |
| Localization | 3 | Only 33 strings go through `FF.t()`. Numbers are hardcoded to `en-US`. |
| Scalability | 5 | The game registry is good. The views are hand-wired, and category icons are split across two maps. |

**Recommendation: improve substantially. Don't rebuild from scratch.** The architecture is sound: classic scripts, the `FF.ui` DOM helpers, the `FF.Games` registry, seeded rooms, and offline-first loading. So are the art and the game logic. What fails the bar is the **design system, the shell and navigation, discovery, the launch flow, accessibility, and the inline-styled game UIs**. Those get rebuilt.

## 2. Most important problems, by severity

**Critical**
1. **Mobile header uses about 210 px of 812 and is sticky.** It stacks three rows: brand, tools, and an icon-only nav. Nav labels are hidden under 480 px (`styles.css:783`).
2. **Arrow keys are blocked inside text inputs and sliders.** `navigation.js:156` calls `preventDefault` on arrows with no `_isTextEditing()` check, so you can't move the cursor in a name field or adjust the volume slider by keyboard.
3. **A fake selection appears on touch devices.** `Nav.focusFirst` adds `.focused` (a ring plus `scale(1.03)`) to the first button on every render. In a quiz, answer A looks pre-selected (seen live on "Aztec").
4. **Contrast fails WCAG AA.** White text on `#8b5cf6` is about 4.2:1. Accents green `#10b981` (about 2.5:1), orange (about 2.8:1) and gold all fail as filled-button backgrounds.

**High**

5. **No search or filters beyond category.** Nothing lets you pick by "how many of us / how long". Games whose cover art is missing show a placeholder, and Bible shows emoji instead of icons.
6. **Launch friction.** The Intro screen has 4–5 ALL CAPS buttons, then a long setup modal follows. Start and Settings are duplicates (`registry.js:419-421`).
7. **The in-game bar has 38 px targets, and Restart sits next to Pause.** One mis-tap wipes the game. Host controls repeat Pause and Restart.
8. **Answer feedback is colour-only.** Quiz options use hardcoded green and red (`quizGames.js:104-110`), with no icon or text cue for colour-blind players.
9. **Modals aren't dialogs.** They have no `role="dialog"`, focus trap, Esc handling, focus return or close button. Toasts aren't announced.
10. **The language picker oversells.** It offers 7 languages, but about 95% of the UI stays English.

**Medium**

11. The Home hero repeats "FAMILY FUN" three times: header logo, a 148 px hero logo, and the H1.
12. On phones the 10 category tiles take about 2 screens.
13. There are about 300 inline `style` sites across the game files, 6 injected `<style>` blocks, and 10 game classes that are used but never defined (`ffv-*`, `puzzle-tile`, `scene-item`…).
14. The global `button { margin: 2px }` hack, `transform: scale` on focus, bouncy hover overshoot, and infinite bounce and pulse animations.
15. Settings show power-user options to everyone (Icon style: Custom/Phosphor). Reset All Data is one tap from a button row.
16. There's no `h1` on inner pages, no skip link, and the favourite button has no `aria-pressed` or game name.

## 3. Page-by-page audit (abridged)

- **Header/nav:** too tall on phones, labels hidden, and theme, language and fullscreen take prime space. Fullscreen doesn't work on iOS.
- **Home:** the art is strong, but the hero eats the first screen. "Play again" sits at the bottom although it's the most useful rail. The featured IDs are hardcoded (`app.js:476`).
- **Games:** the 11-chip filter wraps onto 2–3 rows on phones. The banner repeats the title. Descriptions are hidden in the grid (`styles.css:306`). There is no search, sort, result filters or empty state.
- **Game intro + setup:** two screens for one decision. The setup modal has player chips 1–8 plus a roster plus options, all at once.
- **Game shell:** the title wraps to 2 lines, the bar is crowded, and the score strip is fine. The pause overlay is decent.
- **Results:** the hierarchy is good. The infinite bounce and ALL CAPS buttons should go.
- **Players/Teams:** functional. The colour picker gives no selected state or names, and team assignment uses mini-buttons with emoji.
- **Scores / Favorites:** thin standalone pages that belong inside other views.
- **Settings:** one long flat list with no danger zone. Toggles lack `role="switch"`.
- **Online rooms:** styles are injected from JS and not tokenised. The flow itself is good (code + QR).

## 4. Competitive benchmark

What I'm borrowing, and why:

- **Kahoot:** answer tiles marked with shapes and letters, readable by colour-blind players and pre-readers.
- **Jackbox:** huge type for a room, and "phones as controllers" simplicity.
- **Poki / CrazyGames:** search-first discovery, "recently played" first, dense thumbnails.
- **PBS Kids / Toca Boca:** icon-plus-label navigation, big targets, no dark patterns.

Family Fun's differentiators to lean into:

- Offline and private, with no accounts.
- Same-room TV mode plus cross-device rooms.
- Mixed-age play.
- Faith (Bible) content.
- Swahili and Shona, which reach families most platforms ignore.

## 5. Design direction: "Game-night living room"

- **Palette:**
  - Light theme: warm cream background `#fbf7f0`, white surfaces, ink `#1d1b2e`.
  - Dark theme: deep plum-navy `#14111f`.
  - Every accent gets a `--accent-strong` shade (≥4.5:1 against white text) for filled backgrounds, plus `--accent-soft` and `--accent-ink` for text on light backgrounds.
  - Ten category colours, each with `base`, `strong` and `soft` variants, defined in one place.
- **Type:**
  - Fredoka for display, headings and buttons; Nunito for body text.
  - Atkinson Hyperlegible becomes the "Easy-read font" accessibility option.
  - Fluid `clamp()` scale: 12/14/16/18/22/28/36/48.
  - New **Text size** setting (Standard / Large / Extra large) that scales the root, for grandparents and TV.
- **Shape and depth:**
  - 4 px spacing grid; radii 8/12/16/24; 3 elevation levels.
  - "Toy" buttons: a solid fill with a 4 px darker bottom edge that compresses on `:active`. This feels tactile and costs nothing to render.
- **Icons:** Phosphor and custom SVG only in the chrome, with a `book-open` icon added for Bible. Emoji stay only for avatars and game content. Sentence case everywhere, no ALL CAPS.
- **Motion:**
  - 150–250 ms ease-out.
  - Cards lift by 2 px on hover, with no scale.
  - No infinite animations.
  - `prefers-reduced-motion` and the existing Reduce-motion setting are honoured.
  - `backdrop-filter` only on the header, with a solid fallback.
- **Focus:** `:focus-visible` ring with no scaling. The `.focused` class is applied only when the last input was a keyboard or remote; pointer and touch input set the modality to "pointer".
- **Navigation:**
  - **Phones (<768 px):** a 56 px top bar (logo mark, wordmark, search button) plus a fixed **bottom tab bar** with Home · Games · Online (centre; shown only when Firebase is configured) · Family · Settings. Each tab has an icon and a label, the bar respects `safe-area-inset-bottom`, and targets are at least 48 px.
  - **Tablet/desktop:** the same destinations inline in the top bar, plus a search field.
  - **Family** merges Players, Teams and Stats & medals as segmented sub-tabs.
  - **Favorites** becomes a Games filter plus a Home rail.
  - The old routes `players`, `scores` and `favorites` stay as aliases.
  - Theme and language move to Settings. Fullscreen appears only where the Fullscreen API exists.

## 6. Implementation roadmap (execution order; commit after each phase)

**Phase 0: Checkpoint.** Commit the current dirty tree (25 modified, 13 untracked) as `chore: checkpoint before UI redesign`. Save this audit to `docs/ux/2026-10-20-ux-audit.md`.

**Phase 1: Foundations**
- New `css/tokens.css`: colour, type, space, radius, elevation, motion and z-index tokens, light/dark themes, accent sets, the category palette, and text-size scaling via `data-text-size`.
- `css/styles.css`:
  - Rebuild base, buttons and focus on top of the tokens.
  - Delete the `button{margin:2px}` hack.
  - Keep the old class names working where the games use them (`btn`, `btn-lg`, `hint`, `meta-chip`, `dsp-*`, `seg`, …).
- `js/navigation.js`:
  - Add `_isTextEditing()` and range-input guards to arrow handling.
  - Track input modality: `pointerdown` → pointer, `keydown` → keyboard. `focus()` adds `.focused` only in keyboard mode, and `focusFirst` skips it on pointer and touch.
- `js/theme.js`: add `textSize` and an easy-read font.
- `js/core.js`: `FF.util.formatNumber` uses `Intl.NumberFormat(FF.i18n.getLang())`.

**Phase 2: UI kit.** New `js/ui-kit.js`, loaded after `core.js` and added to `index.html`, `tools/load-check.js` ORDER and the test harness lists. It provides:
- `FF.UI.dialog(opts)` and `FF.UI.sheet(opts)`: `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, labelled title, focus trap, Esc, focus return, close button. They render as a bottom sheet on phones.
- `FF.UI.toast(msg, type)` with a single `aria-live="polite"` region.
- `FF.UI.switch` (`role="switch"`, `aria-checked`), `FF.UI.segmented` (radiogroup with arrow keys), `FF.UI.chip`, `FF.UI.iconButton` (requires a label) and `FF.UI.emptyState`.
- Existing `FF.App.toast`, `toggleRow`, the `seg` builders and modal call sites all move to these.

**Phase 3: Shell and navigation** (`js/app.js` `buildShell`, `_syncThemeUI`, `render`; `css/styles.css`)
- The compact header, bottom tab bar, desktop inline nav and search entry point described in §5.
- A skip link, one `h1` per view, `aria-current`, and the route aliases.
- Footer trimmed: Privacy line, Credits and About. Version is read from one constant (it currently says v1.1.0 in the footer but 1.0.0 in About).

**Phase 4: Home**
- A compact hero, at most about 45% of a phone screen: greeting, a primary **Quick play** button, then Game night and Play together.
- The duplicate big logo goes.
- Rail order:
  1. Play again (recents)
  2. Featured
  3. Categories, as a single horizontal scroll row on phones and a grid on desktop
  4. Quick games (≤3 min)
  5. Play online (`FF.RoomUI.ONLINE_GAMES`)
  6. Favorites
- Featured becomes data-driven: a `featured: true` flag on game definitions, replacing the hardcoded IDs in `app.js:476`.
- Unify category metadata: `FF.Categories` gains an `iconKey`, and `FF.Icons.category` reads from it.

**Phase 5: Games library**
- A sticky search field (matching name, description, category and tags) and one horizontally scrolling row of category chips.
- A **Filters** sheet:
  - Players: "Works for our N players", defaulting to the current roster size, plus Solo, 2–4 and 5+
  - Time: ≤3, ≤5 or any
  - Difficulty
  - Online-ready
  - Favorites
- Sort options: Recommended, A–Z, Shortest and Recently played. A results count, plus an empty state with "Clear filters".
- Card redesign:
  - The whole card is a `<button>` with an accessible name.
  - A separate 44 px favourite toggle with `aria-pressed` and the game's name.
  - Players and minutes chips, plus an "Online" badge.
  - A 1–2 line description from tablet width up.
- **Kill the 404s:** a new `tools/gen-asset-manifest.js` writes `js/data/assetManifest.js` listing the existing `covers`, `categories` and `ui` art. `FF.Assets.artUrl` returns `none` for missing files, so the designed fallback (a category wash plus a large icon pattern) shows without network errors.

**Phase 6: Launch flow** (`registry.js` `showIntro`, `showHowTo`, `showGameSettings`)
- Merge these into one **game details / setup** screen:
  - An art header with the title, description and meta chips.
  - An inline, collapsible "How to play".
  - A "Who's playing" row with roster avatars and an edit link that expands the existing roster editor.
  - An "Options" disclosure for mode, difficulty, timer and TV display, with defaults remembered.
  - A sticky **Play** button.
- One tap from card to details to game. Quick play still skips straight in.

**Phase 7: In-game shell, pause and results** (`registry.js` `buildShell`, `showResults`, `showDisplayResults`, `pause`, GameNight `showRoundResults`)
- **Top bar:**
  - Close (asks for confirmation), a single-line truncated title, a timer pill and a Pause button.
  - The voice button uses SVG icons with an `aria-pressed` state. All targets ≥44 px.
- **Pause menu:** absorbs Restart, How to play and End game, which removes the host panel and the standalone Restart button.
- **Score strip:** one scrollable line. The active player gets a "Your turn" label, and turn changes are announced through the live region.
- **Results:** no infinite bounce, and sentence-case actions: Play again (primary), Choose another game, Home or Back to room.

**Phase 8: In-game component kit**
- New `css/games.css` and `js/gameUI.js` (`FF.GameUI`). Building blocks:
  - `stage`, `prompt`, `roundMeta` pills
  - `options(list, onPick)`: a responsive grid with A/B/C/D markers
  - `markOption(btn, 'correct'|'wrong'|'dim')` with ✓/✗ icons and text
  - `feedback(type, text)` (live region), `inputRow`, `keypad`, `tile`, `meter`, `countdown`
- Move the injected `<style>` blocks from `advance.js`, `creativeGames.js`, `memoryGames.js` and `partyGames.js` into `css/games.css`. Move the ones from `roomUI.js` and `raceUI.js` into a new `css/room.css`. All are rewritten on tokens, and all JS injection is removed.
- Define the missing classes: `ffv-*`, `puzzle-tile`, `scene-item`, `find-prompt`.

**Phase 9: Migrate all 45 games, file by file, presentation only.** Order by inline-style count:
1. `puzzleGames` (74), `numberGames` (70), `quizGames` (66)
2. `bibleGames` (43), `visualGames` (37)
3. `worldGames`, `partyGames`, `reactionGames`, `creativeGames`, `memoryGames`, `wordGames`

Rules for every file:
- Replace `style:{…}` and `.style.x=` with kit classes or helpers, and hardcoded hex with tokens.
- Convert emoji status text (✅/❌/⏱) to icon plus text.
- Use sentence case.
- **Do not change** scoring, timing, seeding, `FF.Race.forGame` hooks or game flow.
- After each file, run `node --check`, `node tools/test/run.js` and `node tools/load-check.js`.

Per the dev-workflow memory, edit with the Edit tool, not Python string patching.

**Phase 10: Online rooms.** Restyle `roomUI.js` and `raceUI.js` on `css/room.css`:
- A big monospace room code with a copy button, then the QR code.
- The player list as avatar chips.
- A full-width primary CTA on phones.
- The GET READY gate and loading screen on tokens.
- Online lobbies show emoji avatars by default. Photos are confirmed as staying on the device.

**Phase 11: Family, Settings and secondary pages**
- **Family:** roster cards with inline name edit; the avatar picker as a sheet; colour swatches with names and a ✓ selected state; reorder buttons; Teams; Stats & medals.
- **Settings**, grouped:
  - Appearance: theme, accent, text size, easy-read font, reduce motion
  - Language: with the "Beta: menus only" badge
  - Sound
  - Gameplay
  - Advanced: Icon style, Demo mode
  - Danger zone: Reset uses an `FF.UI.dialog` confirmation
- Credits, About and Game Night restyled.

**Phase 12: i18n readiness**
- Every new or touched shell string goes through `FF.t(key, fallback)`, with keys added to the `en` table; other languages fall back to English.
- `<html lang/dir>` updates when the language changes.
- Layouts must survive German text that's 30% longer: no fixed-width button grids (for example the hero's `flex: 0 0 320px`).

## 8. Verification

- **Automated, after every phase:**
  - `node tools/test/run.js` (the 56+ existing tests must stay green)
  - `node tools/load-check.js`
  - `node --check` on each edited JS file
  - `tools/test-seed.html` for determinism of online games (19 games)
- **Browser preview** (bump the port in `.claude/launch.json` to beat `http.server` caching, per memory):
  - **Viewports:** 360×740, 375×812, 412×915, 768×1024, 1024×768 (landscape), 1280×800 and 1920×1080. On each, check `scrollWidth === innerWidth` on every view.
  - **Themes and settings:** light and dark, all accents, text size XL, reduced motion.
  - **All 45 games:** a JS loop launches each one through the details screen, starts it, waits for the first prompt, and asserts no console errors and no horizontal overflow.
  - **Interaction checks:**
    - Play complete rounds of a representative game in each category, through to results and Play again.
    - Run Game Night with 3 rounds.
    - Keyboard-only path: Tab, arrows and Esc through Home → Library search → details → play → pause → results.
    - Confirm no `.focused` ring appears after touch input, and that arrows work in the name inputs and the volume slider.
  - **Contrast:** a JS script computes WCAG ratios for each token pair and accent in both themes (≥4.5:1 for text, ≥3:1 for UI).
  - **Online:** dev-rooms mode in two tabs (`FF.Storage.set('devRooms', true)`): create a room, join, play one race game, then results and back to the room.
  - **Network:** zero 404s on Home and in the Library after the manifest.
- Screenshots of the key views at phone and desktop sizes are shared as proof at the end.
